04 — The Studio

“I started STRATA after a decade designing for other people’s visions.”

Here, every project starts with the site and the client’s actual life in it — not a mood board. The land tells you what it can hold; the people tell you what it should feel like. Our job is to make those two answers agree.

Our team is small on purpose: five people, every project touched by the same hands from first sketch to final walkthrough. Nothing is handed off, nothing gets lost between departments, and nothing leaves the studio that we wouldn’t want to live with ourselves.

Nadia Ostrow
